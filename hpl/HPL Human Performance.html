Hypoxia & Oxygen Physiology

The 4 Types of Hypoxia

Hypoxic Hypoxia
Not enough O₂ in the air
Altitude, low pressure, reduced breathing
Anaemic Hypoxia
Blood can't carry O₂
CO poisoning, smoking, blood loss, iron deficiency
Stagnant Hypoxia
Blood isn't moving
Heart failure, G-forces, shock, cold
Histotoxic Hypoxia
Cells can't use O₂
Alcohol, drugs, narcotics, cyanide
✓ CO binds to haemoglobin 200× stronger than O₂ → Anaemic hypoxia

The Presby- Conditions (Aging)

Presbyopia 👁️

Presbys + Opia = Old + Sight

Lens stiffens → loss of accommodation (near focus)

Starts around age 40-45

Presbycusis 👂

Presbys + Akousis = Old + Hearing

High-frequency hearing loss with age

Consonants (S, T, F) become hard to hear

PRESBY = Presbyterian elders = OLD | Anything presby- = aging condition

Sleep Apnoea

Repeated breathing stops during sleep due to airway obstruction

Symptom
Loud snoring
Gasping/choking during sleep
Waking tired regardless of sleep duration
Daytime sleepiness
Poor concentration
HUGE aviation concern — leads to fatigue, microsleeps, impaired decisions. Class 1 medical issue!
