🌀 Depression Life Cycle

1
CYCLOGENESIS
Disturbance + pressure fall → cyclonic circulation begins
2
DEVELOPED
Fronts well-defined, pronounced warm sector
3
OCCLUSION
Cold front catches warm front, warm air partially lifted
4
FRONTOLYSIS
All warm air lifted, system decays
💡 MEMORY AID
Create → Develop → Occlude → Fade
Frontal Depression Lifespan 7-10 days
Mid-Latitude Depression Lifespan 4-7 days

🔄 Occlusion Types

❄️ COLD OCCLUSION

Cold air behind cold front is COLDER than cool air ahead of warm front

Cold front undercuts the warm front

WARM FRONT loses contact with ground

More common in polar regions / winter

☀️ WARM OCCLUSION

Cold air behind cold front is LESS COLD than cool air ahead

Cold front rides up over the cool air

COLD FRONT loses contact with ground

More common in maritime / oceanic areas

🌍 5 Air Masses Affecting Europe

AIR MASS CODE SOURCE CHARACTERISTICS
Arctic Maritime mAc Polar ice cap (NH winter only) Stable, very cold, dry
Polar Maritime mPc NW North Atlantic Stable, cold, low AH, high RH
Polar Continental cPc Siberia (winter only) Stable, very cold, dry ⚠️
Tropical Maritime mTw Azores anticyclone Warm, stable, high AH & RH
Tropical Continental cTw N Africa / SE Europe Warm, dry, stable

⚠️ EXAM TRAPS

  • cPc (Polar Continental) is COLDER than mAc (Arctic Maritime) – counterintuitive but important!
  • Maritime = high RH (picked up moisture over ocean)
  • Continental = dry (no ocean moisture source)
  • mPc – low absolute humidity but high relative humidity (cold air can't hold much moisture)
