Mach Hierarchy — From Slow to Fast
Below MCRIT SUBSONIC
All flow subsonic everywhere. Compressibility effects begin to appear near MCRIT — pitot over-reads, CAS artificially high. No shocks.
At MCRIT TRANSONIC BEGINS
Local flow on upper wing first reaches M 1.0. Tiny supersonic pocket appears. Weak/negligible shock forms at its trailing edge.
MCRIT → MDR TRANSONIC
Supersonic pocket grows, shock strengthens. Second supersonic region may form on lower surface. Normal shock moves aft. Drag rising but manageable.
At MDR DRAG DIVERGENCE
Shock so strong → boundary layer separates → drag explodes. Defined as dCD/dM = 0.10. MMO is set below this to keep you safe.
At M 1.0 (freestream) SONIC
Shocks reach trailing edge. Bow shock forms ahead of wing. Entire flowfield transitioning. Maximum transonic drag (sound barrier region).
Above M 1.0 SUPERSONIC
Fully supersonic. Oblique shocks replace normal shocks. Bow shock attached (or detached for blunt bodies). Different aerodynamic rules apply.
Key Rules to Remember
✓ SHOCKS ALWAYS
Compress & decelerate. Supersonic → subsonic. They are compression walls — everything gets squeezed.
✗ SHOCKS NEVER
Accelerate flow. Subsonic → supersonic does NOT happen through a shock. That's expansion, not compression.
✓ SHOCK NEEDS
Supersonic flow to exist. No supersonic flow = no shock wave. They only form in M > 1.0 regions.
✗ BELOW MCRIT
No shocks possible. Only compressibility effects (pitot over-read). Flow never reaches M 1.0 locally.
